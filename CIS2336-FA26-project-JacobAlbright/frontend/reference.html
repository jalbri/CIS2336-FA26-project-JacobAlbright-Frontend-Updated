Developer Notes

  • The four pages share the same red header, four navigation links, and dark footer. The mobile menu is controlled by JavaScript and tracks its expanded state.
  • FAQ answers expand and collapse with JavaScript, native buttons, and appropriate accessibility attributes.
  • Five sample rooms match the planning PDF: Rooms 1–2 are quiet study; Room 3 collaboration; Room 4 meeting; Room 5 media/presentation.
  • The booking form checks the required fields, UH email format, date, and start/end time; it shows inline errors without clearing valid data.
  • Backend work is intentionally not included yet. The form shows a validated preview, not an availability check or actual reservation. A later Node.js/Express endpoint will check conflicts and approve or decline requests.
  • All page text and content are concept material for the individual course project, not official operating information.
